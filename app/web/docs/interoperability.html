What carries over from Solana

The integration reuses earlier catalog allocation rules, shared calculation vectors and the batch/hash receipt format. Consistent calculation does not synchronize ownership or balances between networks.

NEAR’s role

Today NEAR executes the test payment and exposes its distribution evidence. The proposed next role is coordination of an approved obligation and its settlement destination. No bridge, Intents conversion or destination-chain adapter is implemented in this demo.

Planned: receive on another network

  1. Bind the revenue obligation to its agreement version and each participant’s share.
  2. Let the participant authorize a supported destination, asset and acceptable fees.
  3. Execute through a tested adapter, with durable tracking that prevents paying the same obligation under another chain or batch ID.
  4. Verify the delivered amount, finality and any refund or recovery path.

This is a proposed workflow, not an available payout option. Custody, liquidity, fees and failure handling require validation. Multiple participant destinations must not duplicate an individual obligation.

What would prove the connection

A completed cross-network test must link source and destination transactions to the same approved obligation and verify delivered amounts and costs. Until then, two explorer links prove separate executions only.
