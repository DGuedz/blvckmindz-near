BLVCK MINDZ × NEAR / Product & scope

One participant sheet. A division people can check.

BLVCK MINDZ is a LATAM music label building a clearer path from a track’s collaborators to its payment records.

The problem we are testing

A track can involve performers, producers and writers. Their roles, agreements and payment information must agree before a payment can be checked. Our hypothesis is that a participant sheet, confirmed at the source and connected to the payment record, can reduce repeated administrative work.

The participant journey

  1. Confirm the agreement: understand who participates and which share applies.
  2. Check the account: connect a reported amount to its source and calculate the agreed allocation.
  3. Choose how to receive: authorize a destination and verify the result.

The NEAR demo demonstrates calculation and distribution of a prepared sample. Self-service registration, individual browser signing, commercial statement ingestion and a choice of destination chain are not implemented.

Label operations and software are different scopes

In a label-led release, any label participation belongs in the applicable production and release agreement. The sample allocation illustrates the calculation; it is not a published price, a universal commission or an approved commercial offer.

Extending the workflow to other music teams is a product hypothesis. Software pricing and a commercial service have not been validated by this testnet demo.

A record participants can understand

The intended workflow lets participants confirm or contest their own line and consult a portable record. Today the operator prepares the sample and confirmations. Public blockchain evidence alone does not establish independent artist control or legal ownership.
